Building your overview…

FVG and Strat-trigger entries require price to have pulled back 40–60% of the most recent swing before firing (shown as the blue zone on each chart) — this stops the tool from chasing a move that's already extended. Every trigger candle is read individually (strong body, rejection wick, engulfing, or doji) and a doji/indecisive candle blocks the signal outright. Once a symbol fires in one direction, the opposite direction is locked out for 10 minutes across all timeframes, so you won't see a buy and a sell minutes apart contradicting each other. A signal also needs at least 3 of 5 confluence stars (structure, higher-timeframe agreement, OTE zone, MACD direction, fresh impulse FVG, sweep confirmation, session quality) and must pass the momentum/MACD safety checks. Stops are placed against the precise structural level for that entry (FVG edge or sweep wick) where available, falling back to the nearest swing point. The risk reference uses your entered account size and risk % only to estimate a dollar amount — it does not calculate lot size, since point value differs by instrument and broker; confirm that with your contract specs before sizing a position. For alerts to keep working with the screen locked, add this page to your Home Screen via Safari's share menu. The Assistant panel analyzes only this tool's own saved signal history — it is not a general AI and can't answer questions outside that data. This is a rules-based read of price action, not a guarantee or financial advice.
